🍕 Eat

Good Food to Try

NYC's food scene is legendary. These four spots are must-tries for every NYU student — affordable, iconic, and right in your neighborhood.

🥙 Food Truck

Adel's Famous Halal Food

A beloved NYC halal food truck and a feature in NYC. Their mix-over-rice platter loaded with white sauce and hot sauce is the ultimate late-night fuel. Absolutely worth every bite!

🍕 Pizza

Joe's Pizza

Just steps from Washington Square Park on Carmine Street, Joe's has been slinging perfectly foldable New York slices since 1975. Crispy crust, tangy sauce, and fresh mozzarella — this is NYC pizza at its purest and most iconic.

🍰 Dessert

Junior's Cheesecake

Brooklyn's most famous export and a New York institution since 1950. Junior's dense, creamy original cheesecake has been called the best in the world. One bite and you'll understand why it's been a city staple for over 70 years.

🍪 Bakery

Levain Bakery

These massive, gooey, crispy-edged cookies are a NYC obsession. The Chocolate Chip Walnut cookie is a fan favorite — thick, warm, and absolutely worth every calorie after finals week. Locations in the West Village and Upper West Side.
