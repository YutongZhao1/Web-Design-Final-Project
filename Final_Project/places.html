Places to Visit

New York City is our playground. From lush green escapes to neon-lit stages, here's where every NYU student should spend their weekends.

🌿 Park

Central Park

843 acres of green in the heart of Manhattan — perfect for studying, jogging, rowing on the lake, or simply unplugging from the city buzz. Visit during fall foliage season for a truly unforgettable experience just a subway ride away.

🎭 Theater

Broadway Show

As an NYU student, Broadway is literally in our backyard. Take advantage of student rush tickets and lottery programs to catch a world-class performance at a fraction of the cost. Seeing a Broadway show is a true NYC rite of passage.

✨ Landmark

Times Square

Touristy? Sure. Unmissable? Absolutely. Times Square's neon-lit chaos, giant digital screens, and electric energy is quintessential New York. Go at midnight on New Year's Eve at least once during your college years — you won't forget it.

🎨 Museum

The MET

One of the greatest art museums on Earth — and NYU students receive a complimentary admission rate. From ancient Egypt to Impressionist masters to modern sculpture, you could spend an entire weekend here and still not see everything it holds.
