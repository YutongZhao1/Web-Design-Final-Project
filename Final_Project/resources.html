📚 Campus

School Resources

NYU offers incredible resources right in the heart of New York City. Make sure you're taking full advantage of everything available to you as a student.

🌳 Relaxation

Washington Square Park

The symbolic heart of NYU's campus. The iconic arch marks the gateway of your entire college experience. Study on the grass, watch street performers, or meet classmates by the famous fountain — this park belongs to every NYU student.

📖 Study

Bobst Library

NYU's stunning 12-story library is one of the largest open-stack research libraries in the United States. With millions of volumes, 24-hour access during finals season, and beautiful skylit reading areas, it's your academic home base throughout your studies.

💪 Fitness

NYU Athletic Facilities

NYU operates multiple fitness centers across the city, all free with your student ID. From full weight rooms and swimming pools to basketball courts and group fitness classes, there's something for every schedule and fitness level. No matter where you are on campus, a gym is never far away — so staying active in the city is easier than you think.

🏥 Wellness

Student Health Center

NYU's Student Health Center provides comprehensive medical, mental health, and wellness services for all enrolled students. From routine check-ups to counseling and crisis support, your well-being is fully supported throughout your college journey.
